Known limits
  • Snapshot, not live. Inventory reflects changes within about 15 minutes of them happening.
  • Only published model-driven apps appear. The default environment's three preinstalled apps stay hidden until edited and republished.
  • Flow owner shows the creator and does not follow ownership transfers. Agents report no modified-on or modified-by.
  • Classic chatbots are excluded. Microsoft 365 Copilot workflow agent flows carry no environment.
  • Connector inventory is in preview, and counts distinct connectors rather than connection instances.
  • Unavailable in GCC, GCC High, DoD, 21Vianet and air-gapped clouds.
